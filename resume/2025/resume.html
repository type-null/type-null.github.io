An archived profile of the research, education, and interests behind Type Null.

Weihang Ren

Archived profile · February 10, 2025. The biography below preserves the author's original profile and its displayed last-updated date. Academic stage and affiliations describe that point in time.

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Industry and Systems Engineering at the University of Florida, supported by a competitive fellowship. My research addresses decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on developing algorithms to enhance the reliability, resilience, and transparency of power systems.

At UF, I am fortunate to be advised by Yongpei Guan. Since the summer of 2023, I have also been interning at Argonne National Laboratory alongside Feng Qiu and Álinson Santos Xavier.

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University, where I was advised by Yuri Faenza on algorithms for matching markets, and a B.Econ. in Finance from Sun Yat-sen University, where I worked with Xianbo Zhou on empirical econometric analyses of small and midsized businesses in China.
