Two dice, three coins, and three cards share the screen in this probability playground. Start with 1,000 imaginary credits, choose outcomes and amounts, and watch a sequence of rounds unfold. The balance chart and round reveal show what happened after each play.

[[embed:1]]

## Follow the rules, then the results

The dice and coins are fair and independent. Cards are drawn without replacement from a new 52-card deck each round; the tool explains its card values and payout rules. Displayed chances are calculated from those rules, and a higher chance of winning a wager does not necessarily mean a favorable payout.

This is a rebuilt version of the 2025 project. It runs entirely in the browser once loaded, with imaginary credits and no account. Session records are saved only in that browser's local storage; they are not an online leaderboard.
