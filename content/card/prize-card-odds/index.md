How likely is it that every copy of a card ends up in your prizes? Change the deck size, number of copies, and number of prize cards to see the exact distribution. The default 60-card deck with two copies and six prizes gives a **99.15% chance that at least one copy is not prized**.

[[embed:1]]

## What the calculation means

The calculator draws the stated number of prize cards uniformly from the full deck, without replacement. “Not prized” includes cards in your opening hand or remaining deck. It does not condition on opening-hand requirements, mulligans, deck searches, or other information learned during play.

Calculations run on your device, with no account or network service required after the page loads. You can also [open the calculator on its own](/card/toolkit.html).
