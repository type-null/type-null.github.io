The original question behind this archive was simple: **what can past release schedules tell us about the next Pokémon TCG set?** A calendar makes recurring months, paired releases, and changes between eras easier to spot.

Compare the pack artwork directly in each month's cell. The aligned calendar keeps recurring release months and paired sets in view across years. Filters narrow the same artwork calendar; select a release when you want its details or original source.

## Read patterns as questions

The earlier article looked for quarterly main sets, additional sets between them, and high-class sets near the end of a year. These are useful things to investigate, rather than fixed rules. An empty month can also reflect a gap in this collection.

The 186 records come from the original page, **not a live or independently verified release calendar**. Dates have month-level precision, missing cells may reflect gaps, and 2026 is partial.
