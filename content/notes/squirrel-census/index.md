Take a little wander through Central Park. This interactive map is full of squirrel sightings: cinnamon coats, busy climbers, and the occasional snack break.

[[embed:1]]

## Pick a small adventure

- Look for cinnamon-colored squirrels in your favorite corner of the park.
- Find the climbers, the runners, or the ones enjoying a snack.
- Switch between morning and afternoon sightings to explore a different view.

Tap a dot for a closer look, or browse the list and see what catches your eye.
