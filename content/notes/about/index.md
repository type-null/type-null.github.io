![Portrait from Weihang Ren's original profile](/assets/images/resume/TLPS-1946-small.jpg)

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Industry and Systems Engineering at the University of Florida, supported by a competitive fellowship. My research addresses decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on developing algorithms to enhance the reliability, resilience, and transparency of power systems.

At UF, I am fortunate to be advised by [Yongpei Guan](https://www.ise.ufl.edu/guan/). Since the summer of 2023, I have also been interning at Argonne National Laboratory alongside [Feng Qiu](https://www.anl.gov/profile/feng-qiu) and [Álinson Santos Xavier](https://axavier.org).

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University, where I was advised by [Yuri Faenza](https://www.columbia.edu/~yf2414/) on algorithms for [matching markets](https://youtu.be/5ijLE1MZ2k4?si=-nyAF7br_o8v28sp), and a B.Econ. in Finance from Sun Yat-sen University, where I worked with [Xianbo Zhou](https://lingnan.sysu.edu.cn/en/faculty/ZhouXianbo) on empirical econometric analyses of small and midsized businesses in China.
