Model assumptions & how the calculation works

These 40-card deck compositions preserve the final model in the original playbook. That page contained conflicting suit mappings, and no authoritative game rules were provided. Results are conditional on this table: check it against your game's rules. Each gold suit has an equal prior probability; the ten cards are drawn without replacement.

For each candidate deck, the likelihood is C(♠ count, your ♠) × C(♣ count, your ♣) × C(♥ count, your ♥) × C(♦ count, your ♦) ÷ C(40, 10). The four likelihoods are normalized to sum to 100%.
